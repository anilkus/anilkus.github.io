About

I study how we should judge what language models write. I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at Mersin University, and one question runs through my work: can we measure, explain and trust model output, especially for Turkish and in high-stakes settings such as clinical text?

I lecture in Computer Programming at Yozgat Bozok University, where I head the Computer Technologies Department and coordinate international academic activities. Over the past two years I have worked with groups at KIT, the University of Turku, CISPA and Masaryk University, and I contribute to three EU COST Actions.

Ongoing research

2026

  • Hallucination mitigation in clinical summarization

    A dual-stage framework pairing small-language-model NLI with UMLS concept mapping and negation detection.

  • Region-grounded verification for medical image reports

    RGKV-X: grounding radiology report claims in image regions and medical ontologies, with an LLM arbitration layer. Joint work with TUIT, Tashkent.

  • A Turkish research-article corpus from HPLT 4.0

    Register-based filtering of 334 GB of Turkish web data on the LUMI supercomputer, yielding about 280K research-article documents.

  • Robustness of LLM-as-a-Judge

    How adversarial inputs shift automatic judges, and how explanations themselves can be attacked.

  • XAI-guided attacks on smart grid data

    Using attributions to design missingness attacks and test the robustness of imputation methods.

  • Cross-lingual machine unlearning

    A benchmark of unlearning methods for NER across Turkish, German and Spanish.
