AI · NLP · Explainable AI

Turning research into reliable, real-world AI systems.

I'm a Computer Engineer, lecturer, and PhD researcher in Computer Science at Mersin University, currently serving at Yozgat Bozok University. My work focuses on NLP, Explainable AI, Generative AI, and medical AI systems, with published research on text summarization and disinformation detection.

I combine academic research with real-world product development. Alongside co-founding SmartLab Technology Center, I'm actively building AI-driven startup initiatives, turning research into scalable and responsible AI solutions. My international background (Bahçeşehir University Berlin Campus, CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, and Masaryk University) shapes a global, innovation-oriented perspective.
